Radio4000 / #203 / Read-only UI comparison

Stay on this radio. Follow the mention when you want.

Clicking a track’s @ko002 filters @source-radio. A separate channel link visits @ko002.

Mock tracks, counts, avatars and channel details. Component shapes and labels follow the app’s TrackCard, FilterChips, ChannelMicroCard and ChannelCard. No data is saved.

Compact inline is the production default. It reuses the existing micro-card beside the removable mention chip, adds no extra section, and stays visible even with zero matches. The mini context row makes the distinction between filtering and visiting more explicit. The full preview gives more identity, but spends the most space above the music.

All three variants hide channel context for ordinary searches and tag-only views. Unknown or uncached channels keep a placeholder and a direct link; they are not silently replaced with an unrelated radio. Preview links open the public channel in a new tab. The app uses normal links so browser history and modified clicks work.
